# WaitWise demo script (3 minutes)

**0:00–0:20 — Problem and user.** “Older patients can get an OPD slip but still struggle to understand the queue and find the room. WaitWise makes the next step clearer. This prototype uses fictional hospital data.”

**0:20–1:00 — Patient journey.** Choose हिन्दी, Register, enter a nickname, pick General Medicine, review, and generate a token. Show the department, floor, people ahead, and estimated wait. Point out that no Aadhaar, ABHA, or mobile number is needed.

**1:00–1:35 — Queue and staff.** Open Staff dashboard in another tab in the same browser. Call next and return to the patient’s My Turn tab to show the simulated queue state update. Explain that cross-device syncing needs a real server and is not claimed here.

**1:35–2:10 — Accessible navigation.** Find a place, set entrance to OPD Room 3, leave Avoid stairs on, and show step-by-step directions using the lift. The displayed floor plan is illustrative.

**2:10–2:40 — Emergency path.** Open Emergency, show 112 and 108, and the warning signs. Raise an urgent help flag and show it at the top of the staff dashboard. State clearly that the app does not diagnose, triage, or contact services automatically; qualified staff assess urgency.

**2:40–3:00 — Honest close.** “WaitWise demonstrates a simple patient-to-token-to-room flow with simulated queues and same-browser tab updates. Production use would require secure staff access, a backend, verified hospital maps, privacy review, and hospital integration.”
