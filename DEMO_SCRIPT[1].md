# WaitWise — five-minute demo video script

**Team:** Sudarshan  ·  **Lead:** Sudarshan A. Patil  ·  **Members:** Shubham and Pruthviraj

**0:00–0:20 — Start with the problem**
“An older visitor can receive an OPD slip and still not know where to go or when their turn is close. WaitWise makes the next step easier to see. Today’s hospital, map and queues are synthetic demo data.”

**0:20–0:45 — Choose a language and show safety**
Open a fresh private window if possible. Select हिन्दी or தமிழ் using the first-visit language choice. Point to the persistent Emergency entry and say: “For warning signs, go to Emergency/Casualty now or tell nearby staff. No token is needed. The app never triages.”

**0:45–1:25 — Register and get a slip**
Select Register. Enter a fictional alias such as `Kamla-demo`; choose General Medicine; review and confirm. Point to the token, department, destination, people ahead, “about N min” estimate, QR code and save/print controls. Mention that no real identity, phone, Aadhaar, ABHA or symptoms are requested.

**1:25–1:55 — My Turn and 3-ahead alert**
Open My Turn. Show the current token, people ahead, doctor status and what to do next. Use the prepared Judge Demo scenario so A-112 has exactly three people ahead. Show the banner and explain that any notification is local/browser-only; no SMS was sent.

**1:55–2:35 — Find a place**
Open Find a Place. Select a destination and keep Avoid Stairs enabled. Show the illustrative SVG floor plan, route, walking distance and numbered landmark instructions. Select “I’m lost” to show the Help Desk route. State that the layout is a sample graph, not official hospital directions.

**2:35–3:40 — Staff and waiting-room board**
Open `/staff` in another tab of the same browser and enter demo PIN `2468`. Say plainly that it is bypassable and not production security. Show a queue, call next, start/complete or hold, and change doctor status. Open `/board` in a third tab. Show updates on the patient screen and board; specify that synchronization is only within this browser profile.

**3:40–4:20 — Urgent flag**
Return to the patient page and press “I need urgent help.” Show the new request pinned at the top of the staff console; acknowledge it. Emphasize that it is only a simulated staff flag, does not call an emergency number or diagnose urgency, and does not replace telling nearby staff.

**4:20–5:00 — Accessibility, engineering and limitations**
Show Settings: language choices, three text sizes, high contrast, reduced motion, assisted mode, Avoid Stairs and read-aloud availability. Close with: “WaitWise demonstrates the patient-to-token-to-room workflow. Real use would require a secure backend, validated hospital maps, hospital approval, privacy review and properly authenticated staff.”

## Recording safety checklist
- Keep video under five minutes and show a working screen in the first 30 seconds.
- Use a private/incognito window to ensure no sign-in is required to view the public link.
- Use two or three tabs in the same browser for queue/board sync. Do not claim cross-device sync.
- If the camera scan option is unavailable, demonstrate token lookup instead.
- If no voice exists for the selected language, do not claim read-aloud works on that device.
- Do not imply that SMS, push messages or emergency contacts are triggered automatically.
