# WaitWise scope
- Product: a clearer next step for a fictional government hospital visit.
- Hackathon: HackCache '26, PS2 — Smart Queue Management and Accessible Navigation for Government Hospitals.
- Core flow: language choice → alias registration → department selection → token slip → My Turn/queue lookup.
- Supporting screens: staff queue, waiting-room board, route finder, emergency guidance, display settings.
- Safety: no clinical triage; emergency instructions and 112/108 contact links remain available before registration; urgent request is shown to staff as a simulated flag.
- Persistence: localStorage and BroadcastChannel/storage events for tabs in one browser only.
- Not included: backend, cross-device synchronization, authentication, real hospital data, SMS, push notifications, medical diagnosis, official indoor maps.
