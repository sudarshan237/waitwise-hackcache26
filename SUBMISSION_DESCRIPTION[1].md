WaitWise is a mobile-first prototype for HackCache ’26 Problem 2: Smart Queue Management and Accessible Navigation for Government Hospitals. It demonstrates four-language, alias-only registration; a simulated OPD token with QR link and approximate wait; My Turn/token lookup; step-free Dijkstra directions on an illustrative three-floor map; a staff queue console; an assisted-registration counter; a waiting-room board; and text-size/high-contrast/reduced-motion controls.

Emergency guidance stays accessible throughout the experience and tells users to go to Emergency/Casualty or tell nearby staff immediately without waiting for a token. The app does not diagnose, triage, contact emergency services, or send actual SMS. All patient records, queues, wait estimates, doctor status, urgent flags and map locations are fictional. Tabs synchronize only in one browser profile; there is no cross-device backend or real hospital integration. Production use requires secure server-side staff access, audit logs, validated hospital maps, privacy review, accessibility testing and hospital-approved integration.
